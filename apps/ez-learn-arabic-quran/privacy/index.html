Privacy Policy for Easy Learn Arabic Quran

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Easy Learn Arabic Quran ("the app") is published by Dr Moslem Annuar ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address. We use it only to create your account, let you sign in, and help you recover access if you forget your password.

We don't collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. The app does not use advertising or third-party tracking.

How your information is stored

Your email address is stored securely on our app's backend, provided by Newly. It's used only to run your account. We don't sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone else for marketing.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app. When you delete it, your email address and account data are permanently removed. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing us at the address below.

Children

The app is intended for learners of all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has created an account without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
