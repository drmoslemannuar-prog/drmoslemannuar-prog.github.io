Common questions

I forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and follow the link sent to your email.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the app's settings and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your email and account data. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Is my information shared?

No. We only store your email address to run your account. See our Privacy Policy.
